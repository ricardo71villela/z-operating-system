# Company

## Purpose
Describes how Z is represented as an Organization, its Companies, Brands and Products — what it does today, how it makes money, and how the ecosystem is meant to grow. This domain is about the *business*, not the *system*.

## Scope
Business model, revenue streams, the Organization → Company → Brand → Product structure, partner and market context. Explicitly does **not** define what the Z Operating System is as a system — that is Foundation's responsibility (see `00-foundation/SYSTEM-MODEL.md`). Company references Foundation's definitions rather than restating them: the Z Operating System is the infrastructure that represents Companies; it is not itself a Company. Does not cover product/platform mechanics — see `50-marketplace` and `90-platform-engineering`.
