## A. BUYING A PROPERTY

**Level: NATIONAL** (Civil Code, notarial role), with **LOCAL** variation in municipal fees.

**Typical sequence:**
1. **Preliminary agreement or deposit:** an initial commitment document, often with a down payment, before the final drafting of the contract.
2. **Legal title check:** verification at the Land Registry (or the older Mortgage Registry, where the transition has not yet been completed) for encumbrances, mortgages, third-party rights, and — for an apartment in a building — the horizontal-property regulation (Kanonismos).
3. **Payment of the Property Transfer Tax (ΦΜΑ):** **must be paid before the purchase contract is drafted** — the declaration is filed electronically via the **myPROPERTY** application, the tax is settled, and the proof of payment is attached to the contract so the notary can proceed with drafting it.
4. **Purchase contract before a notary:** the notary drafts and signs the final contract, which **transfers ownership**.
5. **Registration at the Land Registry or Mortgage Registry:** a mandatory procedure after signing, which makes the transfer opposable to third parties.

**Costs of purchase:**
- **ΦΜΑ (Property Transfer Tax):** **3%** of the transfer value (the contract price or the objective/assessed value, whichever is higher), plus a **3% municipal surcharge** on the tax itself — practically **3.09%** in total.
- **Notary fees:** calculated on a sliding scale depending on the property's value (typically around 1%–1.2% for mid-value properties), plus 24% VAT on the notary's fee.
- **Land Registry/Mortgage Registry transfer costs:** roughly **0.475%–0.7%** of the contract value, plus 24% VAT.
- **Agent commission:** typically **both** buyer and seller pay **2%** of the property's value each, plus 24% VAT, when the transaction is conducted through a real estate agency.

**VAT suspension on new-build properties:** the application of 24% VAT on new constructions (licensed after 2006) **has been suspended until 31/12/2026** under Law 5162/2024, with a significant likelihood of further extension per the tax measures under consideration for autumn 2026 (see the confirmation point below). While the suspension applies, buyers of new-build properties are charged only the standard 3% ΦΜΑ instead of 24% VAT.

## B. SELLING A PROPERTY

**Level: NATIONAL**

The seller must have readily available: the official title deeds (registered), the **Electronic Building Identity (ΗΤΚ)** validated by an engineer, a valid **Energy Performance Certificate (ΠΕΑ)**, the **ΕΝΦΙΑ Certificate** proving payment for the last five years, clear tax and social-security clearance, and a **ΤΑΠ** (municipal property fee) certificate from the relevant municipality.

**Who pays what:** the ΦΜΑ is always borne by the buyer, never the seller. The seller bears their own costs (certificates, tax clearance) and any capital gains tax exposure (see Section C).

## C. TAXATION OF CAPITAL GAINS — A HISTORY OF SUSPENSIONS CONTINUING INTO 2026

**Level: NATIONAL** (Income Tax Code).

The statutory framework under Article 41 provides a **15% property capital-gains tax**, but the AADE public service page for that tax is visibly stale and still describes a suspension only through 31 December 2024. Because this guide is being audited for August 2026 publication, that stale page is **not sufficient primary authority to state a later suspension endpoint as an unquestioned current fact**.

**CANONICAL STATUS:** do not advise a seller that “no seller currently pays capital-gains tax through 31 December 2026” solely from secondary reporting. Confirm the current suspension/activation provision in the latest consolidated Income Tax Code or current AADE/Ministry instruction immediately before the transaction.

**LEGAL_STATUS: REQUIRES_CURRENT_OFFICIAL_CONFIRMATION** — statutory rate 15% is the underlying framework; the operative suspension endpoint for a 2026 disposal must be checked against current law.

## D. TAXES ON ACQUISITION AND ON PROPERTY OWNERSHIP

**Level: NATIONAL** (transfer tax, ENFIA, VAT).

**Property Transfer Tax (ΦΜΑ):** transfer-tax matters are administered under the national framework and first-home exemptions exist subject to statutory conditions. The exact tax base and exemption entitlement must be checked in myPROPERTY/AADE for the transaction.

**New-build VAT:** AADE communicated legislation extending the **suspension of VAT on qualifying real-estate transactions for 2026**. Do not assume this suspension applies to every development without checking the developer/property election and transaction structure.

**ENFIA reform:** Law 5246/2025 introduced staged relief for qualifying **primary residences in specified small settlements**, including a 2026 reduction and further relief from 2027 subject to the statutory population/geographic/value conditions. Eligibility must be checked against the official settlement/property criteria; it is not a general national abolition of ENFIA.

**LEGAL_STATUS: CURRENT_OFFICIAL_BASELINE WITH PROPERTY-SPECIFIC ELIGIBILITY** — verify first-home, VAT-suspension and ENFIA conditions for the exact asset.

## E. RESIDENTIAL TENANCIES

**Level: NATIONAL**, with tax/payment compliance rules.

**Rental income tax — corrected effective year:** from **tax year 2026**, Ministry of Economy & Finance guidance taxes individual immovable-property income at **15% up to €12,000; 25% from €12,001 to €24,000; 35% from €24,000.01 to €36,000; and 45% above €36,000**. The original statement that the new scale begins only in 2027 was incorrect.

**Short-term income classification:** from 1 January 2024, AADE/Ministry guidance treats income of a natural person from short-term rental of up to two properties as immovable-property income where furnished accommodation is supplied without services other than bed linen; other/service-heavy or larger activity can be business income.

**Rent-payment method:** this re-audit does **not** preserve the earlier unsupported exact claim that mandatory bank payment begins on 1 October 2026. Any mandatory payment-channel rule and sanctions must be confirmed against current enacted Greek law and AADE/Ministry implementation before advising landlord or tenant.

**Building-upgrade incentive:** current Ministry guidance describes qualifying electronically-paid building-upgrade expenditure reducing tax over five years up to the statutory €16,000 total-expenditure ceiling, subject to the detailed conditions.

**LEGAL_STATUS: REQUIRES_CURRENT_OFFICIAL_CONFIRMATION** for any payment-channel mandate or future effective date not reproduced in current primary guidance.

## F. HORIZONTAL PROPERTY — LEGISLATION FROM 1929 UNDERGOING RADICAL REFORM

**Level: NATIONAL**, with practical consequences that vary building by building through the **Kanonismos** (regulation)

**The current framework remains essentially unchanged since 1929** — unique among the jurisdictions covered in this series, making Greece the most "overdue" for reform of horizontal/vertical property legislation — but with a **draft law expected to be voted in autumn 2026**.

**The building regulation as the key tool for restricting short-term rental — a recent court development critical for investors:** a recent ruling by the **Athens Court of First Instance** (ΜΠΑ 2937/2026) confirmed that short-term rental is **not an absolute right**, and that a building's Kanonismos **can serve as the key legal tool for restricting Airbnb operation**, under certain conditions — specifically, where the building's regulation explicitly provides for **exclusive residential use**, an apartment cannot be used for short-term rental. The court emphasized that "**among co-owners, the regulation has the force of law**." **This does not establish a general principle of prohibition** — short-term rental remains a lawful activity where the regulation does not explicitly exclude it.

**LEGAL_STATUS: REQUIRES_LOCAL_CONFIRMATION — expected autumn 2026 reform.** The Ministry of Justice is preparing to radically reform the legal framework, with a draft law that reportedly includes, among other things:
- **Facilitating amendment** of building regulations.
- **Faster dispute resolution** between owners.
- **Regulation of issues arising specifically from short-term rental.**
- **Updating the rules** for establishing horizontal and vertical property, so that properties that have remained "invisible" for years (e.g., apartments bought under a preliminary contract without final transfer) can be formally registered in the Land Registry.

**The exact timing of enactment and the final content of the law must be confirmed before any purchase of an apartment intended for short-term exploitation.**

## G. FOREIGN BUYER AND GOLDEN VISA — THE MOST DYNAMIC CHAPTER OF THIS GUIDE

**Level: NATIONAL**, with property-location thresholds for the investor residence route.

There is no general nationality ban on ordinary Greek property ownership, subject to special rules that may apply in sensitive/border areas and to transaction-specific due diligence.

**Golden Visa — official threshold architecture:** Law 5100/2024 raised the real-estate investment threshold to **€800,000** in Attica, the Thessaloniki Regional Unit, Mykonos, Santorini and islands with population above 3,100; the threshold is **€400,000** in other areas. Official housing-policy material also provides **€250,000 special routes** for qualifying conversion of non-residential/industrial property to residential use and for qualifying listed/historic property, subject to the statutory conditions.

**Use restrictions:** qualifying Golden Visa residential property may be subject to restrictions including short-term-rental limitations under the relevant investment category. Do not infer that obtaining the permit makes the asset freely usable as an Airbnb.

**LEGAL_STATUS: CURRENT OFFICIAL THRESHOLDS / REQUIRES_TRANSACTION_CONFIRMATION** — verify property location, minimum area where applicable, qualifying special category, completion/renovation duties and permitted use before investment.

## H. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

1. **Is property transfer tax national?** Yes, with transaction-specific base/exemptions administered through AADE/myPROPERTY.
2. **Is new-build VAT universally charged at 24% in 2026?** No; qualifying real-estate VAT suspension was extended for 2026, subject to the specific transaction/developer framework.
3. **What is the 2026 rental-income scale?** 15% / 25% / 35% / 45% across the current statutory bands.
4. **Does that new rental scale start in 2027?** No; Ministry guidance states tax year 2026 onwards.
5. **Is short-term rental registered nationally?** Yes; AADE operates the Short-Term Stay Property Registry and stay declarations.
6. **How are up to two simple short-term rentals taxed for an individual?** As immovable-property income if the statutory conditions are met; extra services or larger activity can shift to business income.
7. **Can I rely on an exact 1 October 2026 bank-payment mandate from this guide?** No; that earlier date was removed pending current primary-law confirmation.
8. **What is the Golden Visa threshold in high-demand zones?** €800,000 under the official 2024 reform architecture.
9. **And elsewhere?** €400,000 generally, with €250,000 special statutory categories.
10. **Does Golden Visa property automatically allow Airbnb?** No; use restrictions must be checked for the qualifying route/property.
11. **Is ENFIA abolished nationwide in 2027?** No; staged relief is targeted at qualifying primary residences in specified small settlements subject to statutory conditions.
12. **Is the statutory property capital-gains rate 15%?** Yes as the underlying framework.
13. **Can this guide certify the 2026 suspension endpoint?** No; current consolidated law/AADE confirmation is required because the public AADE tax page is stale.
14. **Can building bylaws affect short-term rental?** Yes; review the Kanonismos and current case law.
15. **What should be verified immediately before closing?** Tax status, registry/title, building legality, Golden Visa/use conditions and any local STR restrictions.

## I. GLOSSARY

- **ΦΜΑ (Property Transfer Tax):** the national tax of 3.09% on the transfer value, always paid by the buyer before signing the contract.
- **myPROPERTY:** the electronic application for declaring and paying the ΦΜΑ.
- **Capital gains tax:** the 15% tax on profit from a property sale, currently suspended until 31/12/2026.
- **ΕΝΦΙΑ:** the national annual property ownership tax, with significant reductions/abolitions in progress for small settlements.
- **ΗΤΚ (Electronic Building Identity):** a mandatory engineer-validated document, required for any property sale.
- **ΠΕΑ (Energy Performance Certificate):** the energy certificate, required for every sale.
- **ΤΑΠ (Municipal Property Fee):** a municipal fee, a certificate of which is required for a sale.
- **Golden Visa:** the Greek residence-by-investment programme, with thresholds of €250,000/€400,000/€800,000 depending on area and investment category.
- **Horizontal/vertical property:** the Greek ownership regime for buildings, undergoing radical reform for the first time since 1929.
- **Kanonismos (building regulation):** the building's internal by-laws, which "have the force of law" among co-owners and can restrict short-term rental.
- **ΜΙΔΑ (Property Ownership and Management Registry):** a new registry under construction to record the actual use of properties.

## SEPARATE MODULE: SHORT-TERM RENTAL (AIRBNB) — THE MOST DYNAMIC TOPIC IN THIS GUIDE

**National tax/registry layer:** AADE operates an active **Short-Term Stay Property Registry** and requires Short-Term Stay Statements. A July 2026 AADE decision extending a filing deadline confirms the regime is actively administered.

**Tax classification:** natural persons with up to two qualifying furnished properties and no services other than bed linen can fall within immovable-property income; three or more properties or additional services can trigger business-income treatment.

**Local/freeze layer:** restrictions on new registrations can be geographic and time-sensitive. Verify the live AADE/municipal position for the exact district before acquisition or listing.

**Golden Visa interaction:** an asset used to qualify for the investor route may carry statutory restrictions on short-term use; the residence permit is not a substitute for STR eligibility.

**Building layer:** the Kanonismos and enforceability of building-use restrictions remain material and must be reviewed for the individual building.

**LEGAL_STATUS: REQUIRES_LOCATION_SPECIFIC_CONFIRMATION** — registry, local freeze, building rules and Golden Visa-use restrictions must all be checked at the date of operation.
